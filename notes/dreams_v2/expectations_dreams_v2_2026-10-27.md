# Expectations: dreams v2 (written 2026-10-27, before any v2 run; not to be edited)

At native 10 m a 64 px crop covers 640 m instead of ~2.7 km. Each crop holds one or two land-cover patches, not a landscape, so I expect the v2 samples to be sharper and more texture-like: crisp snow/rock edges, individual field parcels, a few roofs and roads, open water with ripples. I do not expect recognisable landscape layouts. I expect the "snow, but a little orange" averaging to drop because each crop is more homogeneous. The random-tile model should look much more like farmland and the path model more like snow and rock than in v1, but both models will still over-produce bright and flat crops. Novelty *selection* will mostly pick out one of three things: rare-but-real content (dark sea, cloud edges, urban texture), high-contrast snow/shadow crops (the agent's v1 "natural noisy TV"), or near-flat oddly coloured squares. The 2 % cut will remove the worst garbage, but I expect the selected 32 to read as "unusual crops" rather than as a different kind of dream. Novelty *guidance* runs through a 272k-parameter AE trained on 2.7 km views at 32 px, so its gradient is almost certainly adversarial. At λ = 1 I expect samples close to the unguided ones with slightly shifted colour or contrast; at λ = 3 visible saturation and checkerboard/high-frequency patterning; at λ = 10 mostly structured noise that scores very high novelty without looking like anything. My prediction: guidance produces something other than noise only at λ ≈ 1, and even there the effect will be mostly colour rather than structure.
