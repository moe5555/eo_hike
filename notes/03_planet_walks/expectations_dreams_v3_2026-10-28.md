# Expectations: dreams v3 (written 2026-10-28, before any v3 run; not to be edited)

The from-scratch path GAN will train on a few thousand tiles that sit mostly in long, autocorrelated runs of one place (the walker steps ~10 km at a time and only blinks elsewhere on long jumps and bored teleports). With a few tens of thousands of crops that are heavily repeated, I expect it to collapse onto a handful of the path's dominant textures: crisp and convincing within those (field parcels, dune ripples, snow and rock if the walk reaches mountains), but with visible repetition across seeds, early ADA artefacts, and probably some near-copies of high-weight tiles. The fine-tuned dreamer will look more like the base's plain samples than like the scratch model: the same global palette of farmland, desert, forest, water and cloud, re-weighted towards the path's terrain, with fewer outright copies and smoother transitions, because 800 kimg is not enough to forget the 4,000-tile prior. I expect A and B to differ less than A and A0. At R = 0.7 everything will be a well-formed, slightly bland "average Earth". At R = 1.5–2 I expect the most interesting images: plausible-looking textures in combinations that don't occur (rivers through dune fields, field grids on mountain slopes, coastlines with the wrong colour of sea). At R = 3 I expect the generator to break: saturated colour blocks, repeated high-frequency stripes and droplet/blob artefacts. Because learning progress rewards structure the 32 px autoencoder can learn quickly, I expect it to pick these broken, high-contrast images over flat noise, so the top dreams at R = 3 will be "structured garbage" rather than grey mush. For C, I expect the dreams to lean away from sand towards green, dark and water-like textures, and their nearest real crops to land further from home than B's, but mostly in other arid and semi-arid places rather than spread evenly over the planet.
